# Traceable Delivery Management

## WHY

- Problem: the guide required evidence-based delivery but did not provide a portable lifecycle or a consistent completion-reporting format.
- Desired outcome: generated project skills can use proportionate completion reports and, when needed, full delivery records without inheriting another project’s directory, naming, approval, or technology conventions.
- Constraints: preserve the canonical portable skill contract; do not turn a work record into execution authority or duplicate specialized API, data, release, or security procedures.

## WHAT

| Concern | Change | Risk control |
|---|---|---|
| Completion reporting | Define a minimum result, changes, evidence, open-items, and status format | Do not claim completion without observed evidence |
| Delivery lifecycle | Add a compact-or-full record decision and closure gates | Block closure when verification or blocking work lacks a disposition |
| Reusable material | Add a generic record template and iteration-management skill example | Keep naming, storage, roles, approval, and retention project-specific |
| Evidence review | Add manual delivery-closure review | Separate passed, failed, skipped, and inapplicable evidence |

## HOW

- Add portable traceable-delivery guidance and connect it to skill selection, authoring, evidence review, and quality review.
- Extend the generic skill and change-record templates with completion-reporting expectations.
- Add a focused iteration-management example plus a project-neutral delivery-record template.
- Update the onboarding and template navigation so a target project can adopt the pattern only when its recurring work warrants it.

## EVIDENCE

- Review delivery guidance and templates against their stated evidence and closure requirements.
- Check local Markdown links after documentation updates.
- Remaining limitation: record storage, naming, ownership, retention, and approval rules require target-project evidence and owner direction.
